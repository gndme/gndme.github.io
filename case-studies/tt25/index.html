// 01. CLIENT OVERVIEW

WHO IS TT25 CONSULTING?

TT25 Consulting is a professional Vietnamese advisory agency specializing in foreign direct investment (FDI) advisory, investor visa procedures, temporary residence cards (TRC), and work permit compliance for foreign experts and enterprises.

// 02. THE CHALLENGE

BEFORE: MANUAL PERMIT TRACKING

⚠️ Missed Expiry Deadlines
No automated alerts for upcoming work permit and TRC visa expirations.
⚠️ Document Chaos
Client passports, legalized diplomas & notarized documents stored across loose folders.
⚠️ Opaque Case Progress
Clients constantly calling for status updates on government application queues.
Un-tracked Emails ➔ Zalo Files ➔ Loose Paper Folders ➔ Missed Expiry Dates ➔ Client Inquiries
