// 01. THE BRAND

A MODERN LAB FOR THE GEAR PEOPLE RELY ON.

FIXORY Lab was founded to replace the opaque repair-shop experience with clear diagnostics, visible progress, controlled quality, and specialist care for modern gaming hardware.

The brand serves enthusiasts and competitive players using devices such as Logitech Superlight, Wooting Rapid Trigger, Razer, Keychron, and custom mechanical keyboards.
