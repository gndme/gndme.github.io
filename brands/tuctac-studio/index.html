// 01. THE BRAND

FOR COLLECTORS WHO WANT A CHARACTER, NOT JUST A FIGURE.

TUCTAC Studio was founded around a specific gap in the 1/12 collectible market: mass-produced bodies provide articulation, but they rarely capture the exact sculpt, expression, costume, pose language, or battle moment a collector wants.

The studio combines digital sculpting, resin production, hand finishing, and made-to-measure soft goods to turn recognizable character moments into compatible custom releases.

// 02. STUDIO CRAFT

ONE SCALE. MULTIPLE WAYS TO COMPLETE THE CHARACTER.

01

Full Custom Figures

Complete 1/12 releases combining articulated bodies, original sculpts, hand-painted finishes, tailored clothing, and character effects.

02

Head & Body Kits

Character-specific hair sculpts, portraits, neck systems, damaged variants, and conversion parts for compatible bodies.

03

Hands & Effects

Gesture sets, domain-expansion hands, energy effects, regenerated limbs, and scene-specific resin accessories.

04

Soft Goods

Made-to-measure garments engineered for movement, silhouette, and compatibility with articulated 1/12 bodies.

// 03. COLLECTIBLE UNIVERSES

BUILT AROUND ICONIC MOMENTS.

The catalog grows through focused character drops rather than a generic product feed. Releases span Jujutsu Kaisen, Dragon Ball, Naruto, and Baki, with an emphasis on expressions, costumes, hands, effects, and battle variants rarely available in standard lines.

  • Romankey, Windtoys, Vtoys, and Mirrfig bodies

  • Display-ready character and battle-scene kits
