BEFORE: OPERATIONAL DISCONNECTION

Tan Thanh Dung Plastic is an industrial plastic packaging manufacturer operating high-capacity injection molding lines. Prior to digital transformation, Sales, Warehouse, Production, Accounting, HR, and Payroll operated as entirely disconnected software silos.

Sales Order (Paper) ➔ Warehouse (Excel) ➔ Production (Paper) ➔ Accounting (Disconnected) ➔ HR Payroll (Manual)
