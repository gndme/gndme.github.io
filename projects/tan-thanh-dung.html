// 01. CLIENT OVERVIEW

WHO IS TAN THANH DUNG PLASTIC?

Tan Thanh Dung Plastic is a Vietnamese industrial plastic packaging manufacturer. The company operates multi-shift plastic injection molding lines, raw polymer procurement, B2B sales, warehouse inventory, and accounting operations.

// 02. THE CHALLENGE

BEFORE: FRAGMENTED OPERATIONAL SILOS

⚠️ Inventory Mismatch
Discrepancy between raw resin intake and finished goods stock on warehouse floors.
⚠️ Debt & Payment Delays
Un-tracked customer debt balances managed via paper invoices and Zalo chats.
⚠️ Manual Shift Planning
No real-time visibility into machine downtime, scrap ratios, or shift yield output.
Excel ➔ Paper Forms ➔ Phone Calls ➔ Zalo Messages ➔ Disconnected Departments
// 03. SOLUTION ARCHITECTURE

ENTERPRISE BUSINESS OPERATING SYSTEM

gndme architected a closed-loop Business Operating System unifying Sales, Inventory, Machine MES, Operations, Finance, and AI Analytics into one single operational platform.
