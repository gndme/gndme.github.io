// ABOUT THE BRAND

MODERN GAMING REPAIR STUDIO

FIXORY Lab is a specialized technology brand created by gndme. It provides high-precision intervention for gaming consoles (PlayStation, Nintendo Switch, Steam Deck), custom esports mouse modifications, component-level soldering, and automated nationwide logistics tracking.

// WHY FIXORY EXISTS

REPAIR SHOULD NOT RELY ON PAPER & MESSENGER

Traditional repair shops rely on fragmented Messenger chats, paper tags, and un-tracked spreadsheets. Customers lose track of warranties, and technicians struggle with component inventory. FIXORY Lab was created to modernize hardware repair operations into a transparent, queue-driven system.
