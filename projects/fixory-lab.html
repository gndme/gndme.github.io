BRAND OVERVIEW

FIXORY Lab is a gaming gear repair studio founded by gndme. The company specializes in console mainboard intervention (PlayStation, Nintendo Switch, Steam Deck), optical mouse switch replacement, custom modifications, warranty handling, and nationwide logistics tracking.
